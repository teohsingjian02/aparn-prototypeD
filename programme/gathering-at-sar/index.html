APARN Gathering at the SAR Conference

Each year, during the Society for Artistic Research’s conference or forum, APARN members come together to share what they are working on, online, so members who cannot travel can join in.

Next: SAR 2027 in Bratislava

Details to be confirmed 21–23 April 2027 · Academy of Performing Arts, Bratislava

SAR’s 18th International Conference on Artistic Research, “Beyond the Familiar”, runs 21–23 April 2027. Join fellow members to share where your research is now. The APARN team will confirm the date, time and format of the gathering.

SAR 2027 has its own call (closing 25 November 2026) and registration on sar2027.eu↗ (opens in a new tab). The APARN gathering is separate.

5–10 minutes, your way

The proposed format gives each participant about 5–10 minutes to share current research and progress. Work in progress is welcome, and so are visual, performative and sound formats.

From contribution to a 5–10-minute update

Your talk is a short, lively version of the same contribution. Visual, performative and sound formats are welcome within the time.

Suggested timing for a 5- or 10-minute update
PartFrom your contribution5-minute update10-minute update
1 Question and contextSections 1–3about 1 minuteabout 2 minutes
2 Practice and approachSection 4about 1½ minutesabout 3 minutes
3 Progress and insightsSections 5 and 7about 1½ minutesabout 3 minutes
4 Next stepsSection 6about 1 minuteabout 2 minutes

Presentation length is subject to the programme.

The format and programme are subject to confirmation by APARN’s Director and organisers.
