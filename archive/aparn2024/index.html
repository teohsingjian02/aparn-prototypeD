Art as Research: Methods for Synthetic Reasoning

How do we make order and meaning from the world? Two days in Cyberjaya explored art as a way of reasoning: how artworks, as philosophical simulations, produce new knowledge, and how art and technology together can imagine liveable futures.

APARN2024Held12–13 June 2024Cyberjaya, Malaysia

At a glance

Host
Faculty of Creative Multimedia, Multimedia University (MMU)
Format
Hybrid: two tracks, twelve panels of 15-minute talks, a panel discussion, an open studio and a closing gig
Practice
Art, technology and reasoning

Highlights

  • Official launch of APARN at MMU by the President and CEO of MMU
  • Panel on creative practice as research in universities, with Gunalan Nadarajan, Danny Butt and Samantha Jury (University of Hertfordshire), moderated by Yap Sau Bin
  • Six papers published in APARN’s JAR channel
  • Live-streamed on YouTube

Event organisers

  • Roopesh SitharanConvenor
  • James Ly Toong KwokConvenor
  • Kurniawan Adi SaputroConvenor
  • Gunalan NadarajanConvenor
  • Danny ButtConvenor

Listed for this edition. The network’s convenors are on the About page.

Read the original call (archived, now closed)

The symposium aims to explore how we create order and meaning from the world around us, encompassing both passive and active forms of reasoning. We invite proposals that explore how art can generate new knowledge, challenge dominant narratives, and envision a better future by engaging with synthetic reasoning methodologies.
