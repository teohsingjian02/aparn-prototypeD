Step 2 of 4: Prepare your contribution

Fill in the eight sections: about two pages, 400–600 words and one or two images (draft limits for review). You will use this same file for your 5–10-minute update and, if you choose, the year-end collection.

  • Replace each piece of grey guidance with your own text. Keep the eight headings.
  • Aim for about two pages: 400–600 words plus one or two images.
  • Add a caption, credit and permission note for every image.
  • Save as DOCX or PDF, named Surname_ShortTitle.docx (or .pdf).

    On a live site: one contribution record

    A production version would keep a single editable record for each contribution. The same approved material serves the SAR presentation and the book, and revisions update that record rather than creating a new submission.

    Contribution
    One record per research-progress contribution, with a version number. Corrections and new progress create a new version of the same record.
    Public content
    Title, authors, affiliations or independent status, country or region, the completed template and images. No email addresses.
    Private contact
    Corresponding contributor’s name and email, for the organisers only.
    Presentation
    Preferred length (5 or 10 minutes), programme status (received, scheduled, presented) and any access or technical needs.
    Publication
    Opt-in preference, recorded on its own; editorial status (not requested, in review, revisions requested, approved).
    Permissions
    Image permissions, consent to record the talk and consent to show contact details, each asked for and recorded separately.
