Design references

References were studied for ideas only. Neither prototype copies their layouts, graphics or code.

  • Journal for Artistic Research

    Observed: Text-first: a bold condensed wordmark, a vocabulary of disciplines, the current issue as an image strip, channels under ‘Network’.

    Idea taken forward: Treat research contributions as first-class content that visitors can browse and filter, linking straight to the published work.

  • Society for Artistic Research

    Observed: A plain institutional layout: one strong statement, artwork beside it, a clear membership button, the next conference promoted in a band.

    Idea taken forward: Keep one primary action (Join APARN) visible everywhere, and give the next conference its own band.

  • SAR 2027 (Bratislava)

    Observed: A strong typographic identity: dates and place first, a countdown, a clear call, text-size controls, a minimal cookie notice.

    Idea taken forward: Lead every event with dates, place and a plain status; make the next step obvious.

  • New Media Caucus, Header/Footer Gallery

    Observed: A dark exhibition space that foregrounds curators, artists and work, with discovery through each show.

    Idea taken forward: Put artists, titles and work in the foreground; let exhibition-like strips lead to the work itself.
